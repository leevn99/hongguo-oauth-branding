Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Tải Hongguo (“the App”) is a personal desktop application. This policy explains how data is handled when you authorize YouTube upload.

Data we access

  • YouTube account identity needed to choose an upload channel
  • Permission to upload videos you explicitly start in the App

How data is used

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on your computer for YouTube upload
  • The App does not sell personal data
  • Videos and metadata are uploaded only when you (or your automation) start an upload

Storage

Credentials and tokens stay on the local machine under the App data folder. They are not uploaded to a third-party server operated by the App author beyond Google’s YouTube API.

Contact

Changes

This policy may be updated when OAuth scopes or storage behavior change.
